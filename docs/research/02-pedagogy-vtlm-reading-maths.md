# Victorian pedagogy requirements

These notes become the generator's system prompt rules.

## VTLM 2.0 (Victorian Teaching and Learning Model 2.0)
All Victorian government schools must have it **embedded by 2028**.

Two components:
1. **Elements of learning**: how students learn, drawing on cognitive science, neuroscience and
   educational psychology. Covers attention, building knowledge in memory, retention and
   recall, and moving to mastery and application. *(Check the exact element names against the
   VTLM 2.0 PDF before quoting them in the UI.)*
2. **Elements of teaching**: **Planning**, **Enabling learning**, **Explicit teaching**,
   **Supported application**. Each element breaks down into 3 to 5 practices.
   - Explicit teaching practices: *Focus the learning*, *Explicit explanation and modelling*,
     *Scaffold practice*, *Monitor progress*.

Explicit teaching in VTLM 2.0 is teacher directed, but it is **not** scripted Direct Instruction
with a uniform lesson structure. Students should know what they are learning and be able to tell
when they have learned it (student agency).

### Lesson plan template (maps to VTLM 2.0)
1. **Learning intention**, from the content description(s), with codes
2. **Success criteria**, "I can…" statements from the achievement standard
3. **Prior knowledge / daily review**: activate and retrieve (retention and recall)
4. **Explicit teaching**: focus the learning, then explanation and modelling ("I do"), with
   worked examples
5. **Guided / scaffolded practice** ("We do"), with checks for understanding
6. **Supported application / independent practice** ("You do"), differentiated
7. **Monitor progress**: formative checks and exit ticket
8. **Reflection / review**
9. Plus: resources, vocabulary, differentiation (support, extension, EAL/D), timings

## Reading position: structured literacy (F–2)
- All government primary schools must use a **structured literacy** approach. Implementation began
  in 2025 and must be complete from the start of **2027**.
- Minimum **25 minutes every day of systematic synthetic phonics** in F–2.
- Generator rule: F–2 English plans that touch reading must use systematic synthetic phonics,
  decodable texts and explicit phoneme–grapheme teaching. **Do not suggest three-cueing or
  "guess from the picture" strategies.**

## Mathematics position
The Department's position statement puts evidence-based practice aligned with how students learn
first: **explicit teaching of key concepts and skills**, fluency and automaticity (for example,
building automatic recall of multiplication facts, which the Level 3 description itself states),
and problem solving that builds on secure knowledge. Generator rule: maths lessons follow the
VTLM explicit teaching structure, include daily review and fluency practice, and use
concrete → representational → abstract progressions where appropriate.

## Other planning context
- Victorian Lesson Plans and Curriculum Companions on Arc (Department login) are the official
  quality benchmark. Generated plans should match their style: concise, with codes cited and
  timings shown.
- Privacy: plans must not contain student names or other personal information.
