# School calendar and planning defaults

## Victorian government school terms

| Year | Term 1 | Term 2 | Term 3 | Term 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 27 Jan (students 28 Jan) – 2 Apr | 20 Apr – 26 Jun | 13 Jul – 18 Sep | 5 Oct – 18 Dec |
| 2027 | 27 Jan (students 28 Jan) – 25 Mar | 12 Apr – 25 Jun | 12 Jul – 17 Sep | 4 Oct – 17 Dec |

Source: vic.gov.au term dates (2027) and a school calendar republishing the official 2026 dates.
A term is roughly 10–11 weeks, so unit plans default to **8–10 weeks** to leave room for
assessment and events.

## Generator defaults (teacher can override)

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Lesson length | 60 min (options: 30, 45, 50, 60, 90) |
| Class size | 25 |
| F–2 literacy block | Includes ≥ 25 min systematic synthetic phonics |
| Difficulty | at level (options: below, at, above, mixed) |
| Differentiation | support, core, extension; optional EAL/D and additional needs notes |
| Output types | lesson plan, unit plan, activities/rotations, worksheet + answer key, rubric, exit ticket/quiz, differentiated versions, parent/newsletter note |
